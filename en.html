OXYGENE MODE

Italo, Eurodisco, Electro-Pop, Synth & Rock

==================

The greatest hits presented in new, original interpretations of the most iconic songs from the worlds of Electro-Pop, Synth & Rock.

Iconic Giorgio Moroder sounds, unforgettable hits from the 80s New Wave movement, popular Euro Disco classics, and the signature electronic sound of legendary bands such as Depeche Mode, Alan Parsons, Alphaville, Pet Shop Boys, Blondie, The Human League, Yazoo, Soft Cell, and Eurythmics, combined with the unmistakable DJ sound of the 90s featuring songs by Gigi D'Agostino, as well as modern hits by The Colors and Gossip.
The music always feels familiar, yet remains unique in its performance
and, above all, irresistibly danceable.


OXYGENE MODE brings the revolutionary sound of that era back to life, taking audiences on a "Back to the Future" journey with fresh energy and the unforgettable atmosphere of the past.

The magic of electronic music instantly spreads through the audience, creating an electrifying "Let's Dance!" atmosphere.
